ID.PROFILE-MARK · A family of marks with eyes: twelve shapes, one accent palette, and eyes that animate the state03

Proposals · id.profile-mark · a family of marks with eyes: twelve shapes, one accent palette, and eyes that animate the state
ID.PROFILE-MARK · mobile · desktop · Profile identity: roster, headers, settings and the appearance picker
A family of marks with eyes: twelve shapes, one accent palette, and eyes that animate the stateBoth x.ai (its row of bots) and OpenAI (“create your dot”) give their agents a face: a flat body with two eyes reads as someone rather than something, and the eyes have room to say what the agent is doing. Today a profile is an origami model and an accent from twelve pairs (common/folds.mjs, common/accents.mjs, PAIR_COLOUR in brand_boards.py); its state is a facet ripple, a dashed unfold or the row’s words. The first step is the set itself, alpi’s own and not a copy of either: twelve soft shapes (kite, gem, onigiri, drop, bell, bean, peanut, cloud, ears, tadpole, bubble, sprout), one flat fill, two ink eyes high on the body, no mouth, outline or shading. The default stays the kite, now a true kite with the longer lower half, so a new profile still starts from the shape today’s kite-base diamond names. The accent palette is twelve colours at one luminance (0.249), so no profile shouts: against every ground the clients paint (light bg, side, card and pane; dark bg, side, pane and elevated) each body holds at least 3.06:1 on light and 4.63:1 on dark, and its ink eyes at least 5.35:1; a custom hex still works and its eyes flip to paper when ink falls under 4.5:1. The eyes are the state channel, the same forms on every mark: idle blinks, working scans, needs you looks up and hops, failed crosses, paused closes, no provider drains the body to 30 % and colours the eyes, offline leaves the faded body; reduce motion holds each still form. Making a profile’s mark is shape × colour × eyes (oval, bead, seed) × at most one accessory (antenna, headset, chullo, flower), drawn flat in the theme’s ink from 24 px and dropped at 20 and 16, inside the shipped AppearanceSheet and AppearancePicker. appearance.next_pair deals the least-worn of the 144 pairs, ties broken by colour order and then shape order, so no worn pair is dealt while any is free; a worn pair can still be picked by hand. Old profiles map fold to shape (diamond → kite, shield → gem, …) and accent to the nearest colour. Workgroups keep their honeycomb in this step; a workgroup mark from the family is a later board. Today the origami alpaca is the brand: the app icon, the wordmark and the default profile’s mark, under the Brand tab’s origami gate (design/AGENTS.md, Tabs). If the creator adopts this family, the app icon could come from it too; the last section sketches that next to today’s icon. Either way it is a contract change approved with this board: the gate would cover only what stays origami, or be rewritten for the new mark. The Brand tab is not touched until then.
