Proposals · ds.segmented · the selected segment is drawn in ink on the phone, as on desktop
DS.SEGMENTED · mobile · Segmented control
The selected segment is drawn in ink on the phone, as on desktopDesktop draws the selected segment as an ink block on the selected tone (design-system.css:53-94, fixed by segmented.test.js); the phone draws it as a pane-tone card on the hover tone (Segmented.jsx:15,37,45). In dark mode the phone's card, #151515 on 4 % ink, is barely distinguishable from its track. The proposal takes desktop's contrast rule to the phone: ink fill, pane-tone label, selected-tone track, the same 44 pt cells and radio semantics it has today.

DS.FLOATING · Floating surfaces on the phone sit on the elevated step with a 1 px edge02

Proposals · ds.floating · floating surfaces on the phone sit on the elevated step with a 1 px edge
DS.FLOATING · mobile · Sheets, dialogs and toasts
Floating surfaces on the phone sit on the elevated step with a 1 px edgeDesktop floats every modal, popover and toast on --bg-elev with the large edge ring (Modal.module.css:12-14, Popover.jsx:63-65, Notification.module.css:18-21). The phone's Sheet, ActionSheet, ConfirmDialog, TextPrompt and Toast all use colors.bgPane with a 0.5 pt ink3 edge (Sheet.jsx:127, ConfirmDialog.jsx:132, Toast.jsx:179); bgElev is used only by the input well. In light both are white; in dark the phone's dialog is #151515 on a #151515 card and never climbs the ladder the tokens promise. The proposal moves those five surfaces to colors.bgElev with the 1 px edge the desktop ring uses.

DS.STATUS-CHIPS · mobile · desktop · State chips and count badges
One chip rule on both clients: 24 px, 8 px dot, mono label, warn untintedDesktop has three state-chip primitives with three rules: Chip (24 px, 8 px dot, warn untinted, tested), .ds-pill (warn tinted 16 %, design-system.css:155) and StatusPill (6 px dot, 4/8 padding, StatusPill.module.css:8-11). The phone's Pill says it keeps in step with Chip (Pill.jsx:15) but draws a 6 pt dot at 22 pt with a 12 pt label (:31,38,42). The proposal makes Chip the rule: desktop's two other primitives render through it, the phone's Pill takes the 8 pt dot, 24 pt and the 11 pt mono label, and common/countBadge.mjs fixes the badge as mono 600 at 14 px / 18 pt.
