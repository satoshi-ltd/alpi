DS.FLOATING · mobile · Sheets, dialogs and toasts
Floating surfaces on the phone sit on the elevated step with a 1 px edgeDesktop floats every modal, popover and toast on --bg-elev with the large edge ring (Modal.module.css:12-14, Popover.jsx:63-65, Notification.module.css:18-21). The phone's Sheet, ActionSheet, ConfirmDialog, TextPrompt and Toast all use colors.bgPane with a 0.5 pt ink3 edge (Sheet.jsx:127, ConfirmDialog.jsx:132, Toast.jsx:179); bgElev is used only by the input well. In light both are white; in dark the phone's dialog is #151515 on a #151515 card and never climbs the ladder the tokens promise. The proposal moves those five surfaces to colors.bgElev with the 1 px edge the desktop ring uses.

Proposals · ds.banners · warnings carry the alert glyph on both clients and the memory alert is tinted on the phone
DS.BANNERS · mobile · desktop · Status banner and alert banner
Warnings carry the alert glyph on both clients and the memory alert is tinted on the phoneDesktop's status banner puts a dot in front of every kind, danger included (Banner.jsx:21), while the phone shows the alert glyph for danger and warning and keeps the dot for info (Banner.jsx:15,48); desktop's own AlertBanner already uses the glyph. The memory alert is a danger-tinted panel with a ring on desktop (AlertBanner.module.css:5-15) and a grey card with a hairline on the phone (AlertBanner.jsx:14-23). The proposal: the glyph for danger and warning on desktop, the dot for info, and the phone's alert banner in the danger tint with the ink4 ring and the icon in the danger text colour, as desktop. Sizes stay per client.

DS.STATUS-CHIPS · One chip rule on both clients: 24 px, 8 px dot, mono label, warn untinted03

Proposals · ds.status-chips · one chip rule on both clients: 24 px, 8 px dot, mono label, warn untinted
DS.STATUS-CHIPS · mobile · desktop · State chips and count badges
One chip rule on both clients: 24 px, 8 px dot, mono label, warn untintedDesktop has three state-chip primitives with three rules: Chip (24 px, 8 px dot, warn untinted, tested), .ds-pill (warn tinted 16 %, design-system.css:155) and StatusPill (6 px dot, 4/8 padding, StatusPill.module.css:8-11). The phone's Pill says it keeps in step with Chip (Pill.jsx:15) but draws a 6 pt dot at 22 pt with a 12 pt label (:31,38,42). The count badge is 14 px mono on desktop with a --badge-size token nobody reads (BrowseModal.module.css:235-248, tokens.css:104) and 18 pt sans on the phone (CountBadge.jsx:9-11), and the kit draws a third one. The proposal makes Chip the rule: desktop's two other primitives render through it, the phone's Pill takes the 8 pt dot, 24 pt and the 11 pt mono label, and common/countBadge.mjs fixes the badge as mono 600 at 14 px / 18 pt.
