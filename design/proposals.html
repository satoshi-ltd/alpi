NAV.FOOTER · The footer holds destinations only: Settings, Notifications and Activity01

Proposals · nav.footer · the footer holds destinations only: settings, notifications and activity
NAV.FOOTER · mobile · desktop · Shell footer
The footer holds destinations only: Settings, Notifications and ActivityThe phone's footer is its tab bar: four equal slots with labels (ShellFooter.jsx:42-150), and the fourth is a theme cycle, a preference that Settings already owns as a segmented control (SettingsBody.jsx:191). It takes a quarter of the bar, it changes the whole app on a stray tap and it is the only entry that does not open a place. Desktop repeats it beside its bell (Sidebar.jsx:698,703-721); there the theme also lives in the command palette's Switch theme (useCommands.js:243-249) and in the default profile's settings (ProfileDetail.jsx:415-419), so a member, who has no Settings on desktop, keeps the palette. Nothing else earns the slot: search and the connection live in the header, New profile and New workgroup in the roster, schedules and runs in Activity. The proposal drops the theme entry on both clients and keeps the three destinations, each with more room for its label; the fold keeps the version beside them.

Proposals · ds.floating · floating surfaces on the phone sit on the elevated step with a 1 px edge
DS.FLOATING · mobile · Sheets, dialogs and toasts
Floating surfaces on the phone sit on the elevated step with a 1 px edgeDesktop floats every modal, popover and toast on --bg-elev with the large edge ring (Modal.module.css:12-14, Popover.jsx:63-65, Notification.module.css:18-21). DS.STATUS-CHIPS · One chip rule on both clients: 24 px, 8 px dot, mono label, warn untinted06

Proposals · ds.status-chips · one chip rule on both clients: 24 px, 8 px dot, mono label, warn untinted
DS.STATUS-CHIPS · mobile · desktop · State chips and count badges
One chip rule on both clients: 24 px, 8 px dot, mono label, warn untintedDesktop has three state-chip primitives with three rules: Chip (24 px, 8 px dot, warn untinted, tested), .ds-pill (warn tinted 16 %, design-system.css:155) and StatusPill (6 px dot, 4/8 padding, StatusPill.module.css:8-11). The phone's Pill says it keeps in step with Chip (Pill.jsx:15) but draws a 6 pt dot at 22 pt with a 12 pt label (:31,38,42). The count badge is 14 px mono on desktop with a --badge-size token nobody reads (BrowseModal.module.css:235-248, tokens.css:104) and 18 pt sans on the phone (CountBadge.jsx:9-11), and the kit draws a third one. The proposal makes Chip the rule: desktop's two other primitives render through it, the phone's Pill takes the 8 pt dot, 24 pt and the 11 pt mono label, and common/countBadge.mjs fixes the badge as mono 600 at 14 px / 18 pt.
