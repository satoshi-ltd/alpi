A soft, moving highlight makes the twelve shapes read as 3D solidsThe marks already turn in 3D but are painted as cut paper, so the turn reads as a flat sheet sliding. A feathered highlight of the body's own lighter tone, lit from the top left, that glides across the body opposite to each turn makes them read as small solids. This is a test of the effect: the edge is soft and the light moves. The highlight is derived from the body, never stored, drawn under the eyes so rule 3 holds untouched, and is subtler in dark. Recommended: the strong highlight, from the 28 px header up, Twin left flat.

The derivation
body = markTone(accent, theme)glow = shiftLightness(body, ΔL[theme])ΔL = { light: 0.2, dark: 0.15 } subtle 0.04 / 0.025 · strong 0.2 / 0.15fade = radial, r 66: opaque to 35 %, 40 % at 70 %, clear at the rim; centre x 38 − 1.1 · 18 · sin(turnY), y 1.1 · 13 · sin(turnX)
shiftLightness is the OKLab helper in common/shapes.mjs that nameTone and markTone already use: L + ΔL, chroma × 0.85 when lifting, hue kept. In light it starts from the deepened body, so a pale accent's cap lands near its stored accent; dark lifts about 0.65 of the light step, so a mark on a dark ground reads lit from above, not lit from within. One new shared function, capTone(accent, theme), beside markTone; the stored accent never changes.
