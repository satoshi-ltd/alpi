Rules
The popover never calls the daemon and never reads ~/.alpi: the main window pushes one snapshot, already filtered by connection and profile scope. It follows the app theme (System, Light or Dark) and is 340 wide inside a 342 window.Esc and losing focus hide it. Open alpi and Review show, unminimize and focus the main window; Review carries the deeplink of an approval or a question. The footer bell is hidden for members.
