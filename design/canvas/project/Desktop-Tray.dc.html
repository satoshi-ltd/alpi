Rules
The popover never calls the daemon and never reads ~/.alpi: the main window pushes one snapshot, already filtered by connection and profile scope. It follows the app theme (System, Light or Dark) and is 340 wide inside a 342 window, with a 1 px inset edge and no drop shadow, which the transparent window would clip.Type: title 20 semibold, summary 14, section labels 14, names 14 in Bricolage 800 toned with nameTone, job 15, meta 12, times 14 tabular in ink-2. Rows are 52 high on the control radius; marks animate only while the popover has focus and never under reduced motion.Esc and losing focus hide it. Open alpi and Review show, unminimize and focus the main window; Review carries the deeplink of an approval or a question. The footer bell is hidden for members.
