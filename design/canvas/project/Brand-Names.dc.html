Brand · names
Names in the UI, solid, in the profile’s tone.Profile and workgroup names are set solid in the wordmark’s face and weight. A profile’s mark carries its accent (in light a pale one a step deeper, from markTone); its name is the same hue one step darker on light and one step lighter on dark, moved further only until it reads at 4.5:1, from nameTone in common/name.mjs. A workgroup’s name is neutral ink, cream in dark, like alpi’s.
