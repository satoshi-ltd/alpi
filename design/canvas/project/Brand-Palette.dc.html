Tones are computed

Light is lifted and shade is lowered in OKLCH, keeping the hue, rather than mixed with white and black, so shadows stay rich instead of going grey. Each colour is one stored value; its three tones fall out of it.

What the numbers say

B has the widest gap between any two colours (8.7 against 5.3 today, about 1.6× as far apart), and its darkest facet clears 3.2:1 on white, in line with today’s 3.2:1. D has more edge but fewer separable hues; C is the gentlest and the weakest on both counts. The twelfth colour, rose, is the one that costs least: it brings the closest pair only from 9.5 to 8.7.
