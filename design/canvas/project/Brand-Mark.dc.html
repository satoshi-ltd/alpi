Where each mark ships. The alpaca is the brand and it is always one ink: the app icons, the splash, the site and the README; the iOS tinted icon and the tray templates are the same drawing recoloured by the system. The wordmark is cut like the alpaca: T3 Crease, three greys with a slit between them, in ink on paper or in cream on night. It is Bricolage Grotesque, shipped as a font in the apps and the site. At 32 px and below the alpaca is drawn with nine facets instead of twelve, the same drawing the menu bar uses in one ink; profiles wear a small object instead of the alpaca.
