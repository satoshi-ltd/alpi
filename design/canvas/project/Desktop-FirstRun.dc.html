First runBefore drawing the shell the app asks the machine for its own alpi: running, stopped, absent or unsupported. On a true first run, with the local alpi never having answered on this machine and at most one saved connection, the window is the Welcome alone, with no sidebar. In every other case the Sidebar stays and the Welcome fills the main pane beside it. Once alpi has answered in a session, a later outage keeps the open view under the reconnecting banner.
