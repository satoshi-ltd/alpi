alpi design systemOne token file, common/tokens.mjs, feeds both clients: desktop reads it as CSS custom properties (tokens.css, parity-tested), mobile through useTheme(), with no per-client override. Six type sizes with a 12 px floor, one family per role, one 4-pt space grid, five radii that say what a thing does, three heights and four surfaces that are the same on both clients. The brand accent is the neutral ink: near-black on light, cream on dark.

Hover is colour only: the state fill fades in over 120 ms on the enter curve and out over 130 ms on the exit curve, with no lift, scale or shadow; pressed is instant; reduced motion drops the transition. Focus is a 2 px ring at 60 % ink outside a pressable, only on keyboard focus (desktop :focus-visible, a hardware keyboard on the phone). A field shows focus as a 1.5 px inset ink3 line; the composer shows none.
