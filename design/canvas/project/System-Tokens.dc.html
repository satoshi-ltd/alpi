alpi design systemOne token file, common/tokens.mjs, feeds both clients: desktop reads it as CSS custom properties (tokens.css, parity-tested), mobile through useTheme(). Geist for text, Geist Mono for labels and numbers, Lucide icons at a constant 1.33 rendered stroke. The brand accent is Continuity amber: deep on light, signal on dark; the brand mark is the tonal alpaca, a golden amber on light and signal amber on dark.

edge is the 1 pt top line of a phone sheet and the off track of a switch; on desktop a small float (menu, tooltip, toast, mention list, model picker, popover) takes a 1 px edge ring and a large one (modal, panel shell, Sessions and Tasks popovers, searchable menu) a 1 px ink4 ring in light, line2 in dark, with no 0.5 px border; the phone keeps a 0.5 pt ink3 ring; focus is the 2 pt ring of a focused field. None is a hairline: all clear 3:1 against their ground. card is the ground of a group of rows and field the well of an input inside it; the phone steps both from the shared value (the · phone swatches) because every screen sits edge to edge on bg. Dark bgElev is #202020 so a floating surface lifts off the pane.
