What every mark must pass.A new shape, a new state or a change to Twin enters only if it passes every rule; tests/test_design_kit.py computes them from common/agentMarks.mjs.
The Brand gate
1One closed silhouette of tangent-continuous curves: no corners, nothing floating, no holes but the eyes cut out of the one-ink icon, menu-bar template and favicon.
2One flat fill on the body: no outline, no gradient, no shadow, no second colour but the eyes.
3Two eyes filled in the paper tone of the theme, white on light and black on dark, at 3:1 or more on the body, inside it in every pose of every state from 16 px; in light a pale accent's body is drawn deeper by markTone until they reach it.
4Equal area and a shared optical centre across the twelve, so no profile shouts in a row of 24 px.
5Upright at rest: a held angle is a turn in 3D, never a flat tilt of the drawing; the app icon is static and may show the held turn.
6Readable at 16 px: the silhouette and both eyes survive the menu bar and the smallest row.
7Every state has a still form; motion only from 20 px and never under reduce motion.
8Never a copy of a known character, mascot or logo, and nothing that reads as a hate symbol.
9alpi is always neutral ink, cream in dark, never an accent; a profile is always one of the 144 shape × accent pairs.
