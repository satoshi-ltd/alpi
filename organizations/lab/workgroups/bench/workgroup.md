The bench produces a short decision memo per task. Each task poses one
concrete question; the group converges on a one-page answer and the
reasoning that backs it. Hard constraints: any claim that rests on an
external fact carries a source, any real tradeoff is laid out as the
options and the axis that decides them, and the final memo fits on one
page. Done is a locked memo with a committed answer — not a transcript
of the discussion. A question with no genuine tradeoff or no external
dimension is answered all the same; not every angle applies to every
question, and saying so plainly is a contribution.
