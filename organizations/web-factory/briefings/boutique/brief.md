## Quick facts

- Family-run hotel in Cádiz, Andalucía (Spain).
- 18 rooms across two restored 19th-century buildings.
- Walking distance to La Caleta beach (~3 min, owner says "180 metres").
- Founded 1947 by María's grandmother Carmen. María is third generation,
  took over from her mother in 2018.
- Restored the buildings 2021 — closed for 8 months, reopened May 2022.
- Operating year-round. Average occupancy ~72%, higher in summer.

## What the hotel wants

- A website that "doesn't look like every other hotel website".
  María specifically: "I'm tired of those resort sites with sliders of
  palm trees, you know? Casa Bahía has actual character."
- Emphasis on the kitchen — they serve breakfast with bread from the
  bakery across the street (Panadería Salinas, owner Pedro). Pedro's
  bread is, in María's words, "the second reason people stay".
- Tone: "lived-in, considered, not luxury". Wants to feel like the
  guest is staying in a friend's family home in Cádiz.
- Booking widget: they already use Mirai's standard widget, keep it.

## Primary guests (María's framing)

- French families in July–August (60% of summer occupancy)
- German couples in June + September (shoulder season)
- Spanish couples and small groups year-round (the locals' weekend pick)
- "Some Brits in winter, but not many — it's not Marbella"

## Languages

- Spanish (source) — non-negotiable, this is a Spanish hotel
- English — required
- French — required (audience reality)
- German — required (audience reality)
- Italian — "would be nice, but I trust your judgement"

## Photography

- Photo shoot by Juan Pacheco (local photographer, Cádiz-based). If the
  files are available, they sit in `projects/casa-bahia/assets/` next to
  this brief — local-first, no external links. If that folder is empty,
  build with the starter's fallbacks (no invented or stock imagery).
- Style direction (applies whether or not photos exist): "moody,
  available light, no flash". Editorial — fits the boutique starter.
- One specific room she mentioned: "la habitación de la esquina" —
  corner room with views to both the street and the inner patio. She
  said this is the room everyone asks for.

## Constraints

- Launch target: end of August 2026, before September peak.
- Budget: standard package per Mirai's commercial offer.
- No CMS — "I don't want to log into anything, just trust you to update
  prices when I ask".
- Current site is WordPress, "a disaster from 2017, I'm embarrassed
  every time someone shares it". She'll keep the domain `casabahia.es`.
- They want to keep their existing logo (a stylised seashell). She'll
  send the SVG.

## Tone, in María's own words

- "considered, not luxury"
- "warm but not cheesy"
- "people-led — the kitchen, the family, the neighbourhood"
- "Casa Bahía, not 'Casa Bahía Resort & Spa', I hate that"
- "if you can make it feel like coming home, that's it"
