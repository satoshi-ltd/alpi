  5 min walk to Messe (trade-fair grounds) and the financial centre.
- 140 rooms, 14 floors, one tower (built 1998, refurbished 2023).
- 6 meeting rooms + a 120-seat conference room; a business lounge.
- Restaurant + bar on the ground floor (breakfast, dinner, room service).
- Year-round corporate demand; peaks around the big trade fairs.
