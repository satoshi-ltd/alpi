## Quick facts

- Budget hotel in Madrid, 200 m from Atocha station (transport hub + AVE).
- 64 rooms across 6 floors, one modern building (built 2009).
- No restaurant; a self-service breakfast corner (coffee, pastries) €6.
- Operated by a small chain (3 properties); Diego runs ops for all three.
- Year-round, high volume — average occupancy ~85%, churns fast.

## What the hotel wants

- "A site that loads fast, says the price, and gets out of the way."
- Compete on price + location against the OTAs (Booking/Expedia take a cut;
  Diego wants direct bookings). "Show free cancellation and the AVE walk —
  that's what closes it."
- Absolutely no fluff: "no storytelling, no slow sliders. People booking us
  decide in 30 seconds."
- Booking widget: Mirai standard, keep it. Direct-booking discount badge.

## Primary guests

- Business day-trippers + AVE travellers (Spanish, year-round).
- Budget tourists: French, Italian, German, Portuguese, plus English.
- Heavy mobile traffic — "most of our bookings are on a phone at the station".
