## Quick facts

- Beachfront resort on Mallorca (Bahía de Alcúdia), 50 m from the sand.
- 180 rooms + 24 family suites across low-rise blocks in landscaped grounds.
- Two outdoor pools (one for kids) + a spa + 3 restaurants + beach bar.
- Kids' club, tennis, water sports, bike rental, evening entertainment.
- Seasonal — open March to November; peaks July/August with families.

## What the hotel wants

- "Make people feel the holiday. Sun, sea, the pool, the kids happy."
- Lead with the experience: gallery-heavy, warm, generous imagery.
  "Couples and families browse on the sofa, dreaming. Sell the feeling."
- Surface: the beach, the pools, the spa, the kids' club, half-board +
  all-inclusive packages. "The packages are how we sell — give them room."
- Booking widget: Mirai standard. Show board-basis + package options.

## Primary guests

- German families (the biggest market for Mallorca) — default after Spanish.
- British families + couples. Dutch. French. Some Spanish (mainland).
- Mobile + tablet heavy; lots of evening/weekend dreaming-and-booking.

## Languages
